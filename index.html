HandleDerjyn
ClassDev wizard
First rigIBM 5155

Some call me a wizard, which is generous: I have an engineering brain and point it at whatever's in front of me. I write the code, I'm decent at design, and where my pixel pushing runs out, I art direct.

It started on an IBM 5155, a thirty-pound "portable" with an amber screen, handed to me with a stack of printed manuals. They taught me BASICA, and I made it draw vector shapes across that amber glass.

Since then I've tinkered with hundreds of languages: compiled, scripted and config. I've set up the environments to match, from Visual Studio and GCC to Unreal Engine, Rust and Go, on Windows and Linux alike.

Today I keep dozens of projects open and hop between them. VSCode is the one tool I swear by. The rest I swear at. I work on facts, not flattery.

Reach out about anything, except the game you think I should make.
